Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This notice explains how tidequestgame.com (“the website”) handles personal data. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

Who is responsible

The website is run by TideQuest (“we”). Contact us about privacy at basicsourcegamedev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not use cookies, local storage or any other tracking technology.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, social media plugins or third-party fonts; the Discord and X buttons are plain links.
  • We do not ask you for any personal data, and the website has no forms or accounts.
  • The only personal data involved is the technical data your browser sends to our hosting provider when it loads a page, described below.

Cookies

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so there is no cookie banner and nothing to consent to. The interactive sea on the home page runs entirely in your browser; the islands you draw are never sent anywhere and disappear when you leave the page.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When your browser requests a page, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request details (such as the page requested, the time, and your browser’s user agent) and may log them for security and to keep the service running. We do not receive these logs.

  • Purpose and legal basis: delivering the website securely, which is our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You are not legally required to provide this data, but a browser cannot load the website without sending it.
  • International transfers: GitHub processes the data in the United States. GitHub states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and uses the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.
  • Retention: set by GitHub; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to Discord and X

The “Join us on Discord” link takes you to Discord, run by Discord Inc., and the “Follow us on X” link takes you to X, run by X Corp. These are separate services: once you are there, Discord’s privacy policy or X’s privacy policy applies. The website embeds nothing from either service and loads nothing from them until you follow a link, and we do not share any data with them.

Discord and the Discord logo are trademarks of Discord Inc. X and the X logo are trademarks of X Corp. TideQuest is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord or X.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. As we do not hold any data that identifies you, we will usually have nothing to return, but you can always write to basicsourcegamedev@gmail.com.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Changes

If the way the website handles data changes, for example if we ever add analytics, we will update this notice and the date above before the change takes effect.

Accessibility statement

Last updated: 2 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use tidequestgame.com, including people with disabilities. This statement covers the whole website: the home page with its privacy and accessibility notices, the Discord link page and the not-found page.

Standards

The website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the level referenced by the European standard EN 301 549.

Status: partially conformant. Everything on the website meets these standards except the interactive sea described under “Known limitations”.

What we have done

  • Every page, link and control can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative images and the animated sea are hidden from screen readers.
  • Each page declares its language and has a meaningful title and heading.
  • Text meets the WCAG AA contrast ratios against its background.
  • Pages can be zoomed to 200% and viewed on narrow screens without loss of content.
  • The animated sea can be paused with the “Pause animation” button at the bottom of the home page, and it stays still if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Sound effects play only when you build or wash away sand, never on their own, and can be turned off with the speaker button at the bottom right of the home page.
  • Nothing on the website flashes.

Known limitations

  • The interactive sea behind the home page (drawing islands with the mouse or a finger) cannot be used with a keyboard or a screen reader. It is a decorative extra: it carries no information, and every part of the website works without it.
  • The Discord and X links lead to discord.com and x.com, which are run by Discord Inc. and X Corp. and are outside our control.

How we checked

The website was reviewed on 2 October 2026 by manual keyboard testing, contrast measurement, testing at 200% zoom and at phone width, and testing with reduced motion enabled, in current versions of Chrome.

Feedback and contact

If something on the website is hard to use, or you need information in a different format, please contact our accessibility coordinator at basicsourcegamedev@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
