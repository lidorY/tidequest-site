Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This notice explains how tidequestgame.com (the website) handles personal data. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other data protection laws that apply.

Who runs the website

TideQuest runs the website. For anything about privacy, write to basicsourcegamedev@gmail.com.

In short

  • We don't use cookies, local storage or any other tracking.
  • We don't use analytics, ads, social media plugins or third-party fonts. The Discord and X buttons are plain links.
  • We don't ask for any personal data. There are no forms or accounts.
  • The only personal data involved is what your browser sends to our hosting provider when it loads a page. Details are below.

Cookies

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so there's no cookie banner and nothing to agree to. The sea on the home page runs in your browser. The islands you draw are never sent anywhere, and they're gone when you leave the page.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When your browser asks for a page, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request details, such as the page, the time and your browser's user agent. GitHub may log these for security and to keep the service running. We don't get these logs.

We use GitHub to deliver the website safely, which is our legitimate interest under GDPR Art. 6(1)(f). You don't have to provide this data, but a browser can't load the website without sending it.

GitHub processes the data in the United States. GitHub says it takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. GitHub decides how long it keeps the data. You can read more in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to Discord and X

The Discord button takes you to Discord, run by Discord Inc. The X button takes you to X, run by X Corp. Once you're there, Discord's privacy policy or X's privacy policy applies. Nothing from either service is loaded until you click, and we don't share any data with them.

Discord and the Discord logo are trademarks of Discord Inc. X and the X logo are trademarks of X Corp. TideQuest is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord or X.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to limit or object to how it's used, or to get a copy of it. We don't hold any data that identifies you, so there will usually be nothing to send, but you can always write to basicsourcegamedev@gmail.com.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Changes

If this changes, for example if we add analytics one day, we'll update this notice and the date above before it happens.

Accessibility statement

Last updated: 2 October 2026

We want tidequestgame.com to work for everyone, including people with disabilities. This statement covers the whole website: the home page with these notices, the Discord link page and the not-found page.

Standards

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, which is the level the European standard EN 301 549 refers to.

The website partially meets this standard. The one exception is the interactive sea, described under Known limitations below.

What we've done

  • You can reach and use every page, link and control with a keyboard, and the focus outline is easy to see.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives. Decorative images and the animated sea are hidden from screen readers.
  • Each page sets its language and has a clear title and heading.
  • Text meets the WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • You can zoom pages to 200% or view them on a narrow screen without losing content.
  • You can pause the animated sea with the Pause animation button at the bottom of the home page. It also stays still if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Sound effects only play when you build or wash away sand, never on their own. You can turn them off with the speaker button at the bottom right of the home page.
  • Nothing on the website flashes.

Known limitations

  • You can't draw islands on the sea with a keyboard or a screen reader. The sea is decorative, carries no information, and the rest of the website works without it.
  • The Discord and X links go to discord.com and x.com, which Discord Inc. and X Corp. run. We don't control them.

How we checked

We reviewed the website on 2 October 2026 with a keyboard, contrast measurements, 200% zoom, a phone-width screen and reduced motion turned on, in current versions of Chrome.

Feedback and contact

If something is hard to use, or you need information in another format, contact our accessibility coordinator at basicsourcegamedev@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
